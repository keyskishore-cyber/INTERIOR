About R G Agency

Crafting Interiors That Reflect Your Story

R G Agency is a Coimbatore-based interior design studio dedicated to transforming houses and offices into refined, functional spaces. From concept to completion, our designers blend clean aesthetics with practical craftsmanship to create interiors that feel personal and premium.

We specialize in modular kitchens, wardrobes, false ceilings, doors and complete renovation works — managing every detail so you experience a smooth, stress-free journey from first sketch to final handover.
